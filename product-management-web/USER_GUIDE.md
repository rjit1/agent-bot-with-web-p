## ➕ **Adding a New Product**

### **Step 1: Click "Add Product"**
- Look for the **blue "+" button** in the bottom-right corner
- Click it to open the "Add New Product" form

### **Step 2: Fill in Basic Information**

#### **Product ID** (Required)
- **What it is**: A unique identifier for your product
- **Example**: `PROD-001`, `TOY-123`, `ELEC-456`
- **Tip**: Use a simple format like `CATEGORY-NUMBER`

#### **Category** (Required)
- **What it is**: The type of product you're selling
- **How to use**: 
  - Click the dropdown to see common categories
  - Select from the list OR type your own category
- **Common categories**: Electronics, Toys & Games, Clothing, etc.

#### **Product Title** (Required)
- **What it is**: The name of your product
- **Example**: "Wireless Bluetooth Headphones", "Educational Building Blocks"

#### **Description** (Required)
- **What it is**: Detailed information about your product
- **Example**: "High-quality wireless headphones with noise cancellation and 8-hour battery life"

### **Step 3: Set Pricing**

#### **Price** (Required)
- **What it is**: How much you're selling the product for
- **Example**: `1000` (for ₹1000)
- **Tip**: Enter numbers only, no currency symbols

#### **Discount Price** (Optional)
- **What it is**: Special sale price (if any)
- **Example**: `800` (for ₹800)
- **Leave blank** if no discount

### **Step 4: Add Specifications** (Easy Way!)

#### **What are Specifications?**
- **Definition**: Key details about your product
- **Examples**: Battery life, size, weight, material, etc.

#### **How to Add Specifications:**

1. **Click "Add Specification"**
2. **Fill in two fields**:
   - **Specification Name**: What you're describing (e.g., "Battery Life")
   - **Value**: The actual detail (e.g., "8 hours")

3. **Examples**:
   ```
   Specification Name: Battery Life    Value: 8 hours
   Specification Name: Weight          Value: 200g
   Specification Name: Material        Value: Plastic
   Specification Name: Age Range       Value: 3-8 years
   ```

4. **Quick Start**: If you selected a category, click **"Load Common Specs"** to get pre-filled examples!

#### **Common Specifications by Category:**

**Electronics:**
- Battery Life: 8 hours
- Screen Size: 6.1 inches
- Storage: 128GB
- RAM: 8GB

**Toys & Games:**
- Age Range: 3-8 years
- Material: Plastic
- Battery Required: No
- Assembly Required: No

**Clothing:**
- Size: Medium
- Material: Cotton
- Care Instructions: Machine washable
- Color: Blue

### **Step 5: Upload Product Images**

#### **How to Upload Images:**
1. **Drag and drop** images onto the upload area
2. **OR click** the upload area to select files
3. **Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
4. **Maximum size**: 5MB per image
5. **Multiple images**: You can upload several at once

#### **Image Tips:**
- **Use clear, well-lit photos**
- **Show the product from different angles**
- **Make sure the product is the main focus**

### **Step 6: Set Stock Status**
- **In Stock**: Product is available for sale
- **Out of Stock**: Product is temporarily unavailable
- **Pre Order**: Product is coming soon

### **Step 7: Add Warranty Information** (Optional)
- **Example**: "1 Year Manufacturer Warranty"
- **Example**: "6 Months Warranty"

### **Step 8: Save Your Product**
- Click **"Save Product"** button
- Your product will be added to the system!

## 🔍 **Finding Products**

### **Search by Product ID or Title:**
1. Type in the search box
2. Press Enter or click "Search"
3. Results will show matching products

### **Filter by Category:**
1. Select a category from the dropdown
2. Click "Search"
3. Only products from that category will show

---

## ✏️ **Editing Products**

### **How to Edit:**
1. Find the product you want to edit
2. Click the **pencil icon** (✏️) on the product card
3. Make your changes in the form
4. Click **"Save Product"**

### **What You Can Edit:**
- All product information
- Add/remove specifications
- Add/remove images
- Change pricing
- Update stock status

---

## 🗑️ **Deleting Products**

### **How to Delete:**
1. Find the product you want to delete
2. Click the **trash icon** (🗑️) on the product card
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted
4. **Warning**: This action cannot be undone!
